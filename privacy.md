# Sudar 隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

生效日期 / Effective date: 2026-09-25

## 简体中文

Sudar 是一款基于 Maccy、由 allonli 独立维护的 macOS 剪贴板管理器。本隐私政策适用于 Sudar。

### 本地处理的数据

Sudar 在运行且未暂停记录时读取系统剪贴板，将复制的文字、图片、文件引用以及来源应用、复制时间、复制次数保存在你的 Mac 上。这些信息用于历史搜索、预览、固定和再次复制粘贴。你创建的文本片段和应用偏好也保存在本机。文件引用可能包含本地文件路径。

### 不上传和不跟踪

Sudar 不向开发者或第三方上传剪贴板内容、片段、偏好或使用记录。应用不要求注册账户，不包含广告、第三方分析或跟踪 SDK。开发者无法远程访问你的剪贴板数据库。Mac App Store 下载、更新及 Apple 自己的服务适用 Apple 的隐私政策。

### 权限

可选的辅助功能权限用于将你选中的条目粘贴到当前应用；拒绝该权限不妨碍保存、搜索或复制历史，仍可手动粘贴。文件访问仅用于你选择的导入、导出或数据目录。Sudar 不主动授予其他应用访问你的历史记录的权限。

### 你的控制权

你可以暂停记录、设置忽略应用、调整历史容量、删除单条或清空历史。固定条目和文本片段可能需要单独删除。导出会在你选择的位置生成含所选数据的文件，请自行妥善保管。若你选择云盘同步目录、使用设备备份或系统通用剪贴板，对应服务可能按其自身设置同步数据；这些服务并非由 Sudar 提供。

本地历史和导出文件未由 Sudar 另行加密，请使用 macOS 账户保护和 FileVault 等系统保护措施。Sudar 不保证能识别所有敏感复制内容，请不要将其视为密码保管工具。

### 支持与政策变更

通过 [支持页面](https://github.com/allonli/sudar-support) 或 [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/allonli/sudar-support/issues) 联系维护者。你主动提交到 GitHub 的问题及附件适用 GitHub 的政策，且公开问题对所有人可见；请不要提交敏感内容。本政策如有修改，将更新本页日期。

## English

Sudar is a macOS clipboard manager based on Maccy and independently maintained by allonli. This privacy policy applies to Sudar.

### Local data

While running with recording enabled, Sudar reads the system clipboard and stores copied text, images, file references, source application, copy timestamps, and copy counts on your Mac. It uses this information for history search, previews, pins, and copying or pasting again. Snippets and preferences are also stored locally. File references may contain local paths.

### No uploads or tracking

Sudar does not upload clipboard contents, snippets, preferences, or usage records to the developer or third parties. It does not require an account or include advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking SDKs. The developer cannot remotely access your clipboard database. App Store downloads, updates, and Apple's own services are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

### Permissions and control

Optional Accessibility access enables pasting a selected item into the active app. Without this permission, history recording, search, and copying remain available and you can paste manually. File access is used for locations you choose for imports, exports, or storage.

You can pause recording, ignore specific apps, limit history, delete individual items, or clear history. Pinned items and snippets may need to be deleted separately. Exports contain your selected data and should be stored securely. Cloud storage folders, device backups, or Universal Clipboard may synchronize data according to their own settings; Sudar does not operate those services.

Sudar does not separately encrypt local history or export files. Use macOS account protection and system safeguards such as FileVault. Sudar cannot identify every sensitive clipboard item and is not a password vault.

### Support and changes

Contact the maintainer through the [support page](https://github.com/allonli/sudar-support) or [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/allonli/sudar-support/issues). Information you choose to post to GitHub is subject to GitHub's policies. Public issues and attachments are visible to others, so do not include sensitive clipboard data. Policy changes will appear here with an updated effective date.
